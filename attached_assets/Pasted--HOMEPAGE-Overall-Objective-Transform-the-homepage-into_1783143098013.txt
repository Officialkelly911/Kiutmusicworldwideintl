🎬 HOMEPAGE

Overall Objective

Transform the homepage into a cinematic, premium landing experience that immediately communicates Kiut’s brand identity while encouraging visitors to explore the music, videos, store, and artist story.

⸻

1. Hero Section Refinement

Keep the existing hero video, but enhance its presentation.

Improvements

* Add a cinematic entrance sequence:
    * Hero background video fades in first.
    * Kiut logo appears with a slight glow.
    * Headline animates upward.
    * Subtitle fades in.
    * CTA buttons appear with staggered timing.
    * Animated scroll indicator appears last.
* Add a subtle dark overlay (approximately 35–45%) to improve text readability.
* Introduce a soft gold ambient glow around the hero title.
* Add a smooth parallax effect between the hero content and the background video.

⸻

2. Premium Loading Experience

Replace the standard page load with a branded loading animation.

Display:

* Custom Kiut “K” monogram.
* Animated gold waveform.
* “Loading Experience” text.
* Smooth fade into the homepage.

Target duration: approximately 1.5 seconds.

⸻

3. Animated Statistics Section

Insert a compact statistics strip between the Hero section and the Creative Portfolio.

Example metrics:

* 20+ Music Videos
* 15+ Singles
* Worldwide Audience
* Millions of Streams

Animate the counters when they enter the viewport.

⸻

4. Creative Portfolio

Keep the existing layout.

Enhance with:

* More spacing.
* Consistent card heights.
* Slight hover lift.
* Soft gold border glow.
* Smooth image zoom.
* Refined hover shadows.

⸻

5. Store CTA Section

Retain the current concept while improving presentation.

Improvements

* Merchandise images should fill more of each card.
* Allow products to extend slightly beyond the card boundaries for a premium fashion-style presentation.
* Increase image scale while keeping products fully visible.
* Standardize all product dimensions.
* Add gentle hover animation.
* Improve CTA button styling.
* Add subtle background glow behind featured products.

⸻

6. Section Transitions

Avoid abrupt transitions.

Between sections, introduce:

* Gradient fades.
* Soft blur dividers.
* Ambient glow transitions.
* Increased vertical spacing.

⸻

7. Motion System

Every homepage section should animate into view.

Use:

* Fade.
* Slide.
* Scale.
* Staggered reveal.

Animations should remain smooth and subtle.

⸻

8. Interactive Background

Add very subtle ambient lighting behind major sections.

Examples:

* Floating blurred circles.
* Gold light streaks.
* Slow-moving gradients.

Opacity should remain below 5%.

⸻

9. Floating Music Player

Add a persistent mini-player across the website.

Features:

* Album artwork.
* Song title.
* Play/Pause.
* Progress bar.
* Next track.
* Volume control (optional).
* Minimize/Expand.

The player should never obstruct important content and should remain responsive.

⸻

10. Featured Quote

Introduce a minimalist artist quote between major homepage sections.

Example:

“Music is more than sound. It’s memory.”

Animate gently into view.

⸻

11. Global Consistency Pass

Ensure all homepage components share:

* Identical border radius.
* Consistent spacing.
* Matching shadows.
* Unified typography scale.
* Consistent animation timing.
* Uniform button styles.

⸻

🎵 MUSIC PAGE

Overall Objective

Transform the Music page into a premium digital streaming hub that encourages exploration while maintaining a clean, elegant experience.

⸻

1. Album Card Redesign

Retain the current layout.

Upgrade every release card with:

* Glassmorphism styling.
* Larger album artwork.
* Gradient borders.
* Better spacing.
* Improved typography hierarchy.

⸻

2. Premium Hover Effects

On hover:

* Card lifts slightly.
* Artwork slowly zooms.
* Soft gold glow appears.
* Shadow increases.
* Play button animates.

⸻

3. Streaming Platform Badges

Display platform availability beneath every release.

Supported platforms:

* Spotify
* Apple Music
* Audiomack
* YouTube Music
* Boomplay (if available)

Use branded icons.

⸻

4. Album Metadata

Display additional release information.

Include:

* Number of tracks.
* Release year.
* Genre.
* Available platforms.
* Official Release badge.

⸻

5. Animated Timeline

Retain the existing timeline.

Enhance with:

* Scroll-triggered animations.
* Sequential node activation.
* Smooth connecting line animation.

⸻

6. Floating Audio Equalizer

Introduce a subtle animated waveform within the page background.

Keep movement slow and understated.

⸻

7. Background Enhancement

Maintain the black background while introducing:

* Soft floating gold particles.
* Ambient gradients.
* Gentle light movement.

Opacity should remain below 5%.

⸻

8. CTA Button Refinement

Replace generic labels such as “Play Now” with:

* ▶ Listen Now
* ▶ Stream Everywhere

Buttons should feature:

* Gradient backgrounds.
* Hover glow.
* Press animation.
* Ripple effect.

⸻

9. Listening Progress

If playback history is available, display:

“Continue Listening”

instead of

“Play Now”

where appropriate.

⸻

10. Music Discovery Section

Near the bottom of the page, introduce a recommendation area.

Example:

“If you enjoyed Good Life EP”

“You may also like”

Display related singles or albums.

⸻

11. Motion Design

Animate:

* Album cards.
* Timeline.
* Buttons.
* Headers.
* Platform badges.

Maintain smooth, premium motion throughout.

⸻

12. Visual Consistency

Ensure all music page elements follow the same design language established across the website.
