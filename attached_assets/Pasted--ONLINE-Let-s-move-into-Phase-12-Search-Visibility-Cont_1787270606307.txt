Objective

Implement Phase 12 of the KIUT Music SEO roadmap.

The purpose of this phase is to improve search discoverability, semantic relevance, internal linking, music/release discoverability, entity understanding, and content quality while preserving the existing KIUT visual identity and all successfully completed Phase 11 functionality.

This is an SEO/content architecture implementation phase, not a redesign.
