Performance

Use SVG whenever possible.

Lazy-load large assets.

Preload navigation logos.

Optimize PNG fallbacks.

Avoid layout shifts.

Maintain crisp rendering on Retina displays.

All animations must run at 60 FPS.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion by reducing or disabling non-essential animations.

Final Acceptance Criteria

The implementation is complete only when:

* The uploaded Monogram is used exactly as the official Brand Mark.
* The uploaded Full Lockup is used exactly as the Primary Logo.
* No AI-generated or recreated logos exist anywhere in the project.
* Navigation, hero, footer, and mobile layouts follow the defined hierarchy.
* The cinematic loading animation is implemented.
* Premium gold CTA buttons with hover and touch interactions are applied consistently.
* Monogram watermarks appear tastefully throughout the site.
* Favicons, app icons, and social profile assets are generated from the Monogram.
* All branding is visually consistent, responsive, accessible, and optimized for performance.

The end result should feel comparable to the digital experience of a luxury fashion house or premium music label: refined, restrained, elegant, and unmistakably KIUT Music.
