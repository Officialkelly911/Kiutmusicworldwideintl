KIUT Music Brand Integration — Phase 7

Motion & Animation System

Objective

Create a unified motion design system across the entire Kiut Music website.

Do not redesign layouts.

Do not introduce unnecessary animations.

The goal is to make the website feel premium, cinematic, and fluid while maintaining excellent performance.

⸻

Global Motion Principles

Every animation should feel:

• Elegant

• Smooth

• Deliberate

• Premium

• Cinematic

Avoid animations that feel playful, exaggerated, or distracting.

⸻

Animation Standards

Use one global animation timing system.

Default Duration

200–300ms

Large Section Transitions

500–700ms

Hero Elements

700–1200ms

Use easing similar to:

ease-in-out

or

cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)

Remove inconsistent animation timings throughout the project.

⸻

Hero Section

Apply staggered entrance animations.

Sequence:

1. Background video fades in.
2. Dark overlay fades in.
3. Logo appears.
4. Headline fades upward.
5. Subtitle fades upward.
6. CTA buttons appear last.

Do not animate everything simultaneously.

⸻

Navigation

On page load:

• Fade in smoothly.

On scroll:

• Transition into a slightly smaller sticky navigation.

• Add a subtle backdrop blur.

• Maintain smooth transitions.

Hover:

• Gold underline animation.

• Smooth color transition.

⸻

Buttons

Hover:

• Lift upward 2–4px.

• Slight shadow increase.

• Smooth gold transition.

Active:

• Gentle press effect.

Focus:

• Accessible focus ring.

⸻

Cards

Gallery

Music Releases

Store

Portfolio

News

Apply:

• Slight lift

• Shadow increase

• Gentle image zoom (maximum 1.05 scale)

No excessive bouncing.

⸻

Gallery

Hover:

• Cinematic zoom.

• Soft dark overlay.

• Light gold accent.

Lightbox:

• Smooth fade.

• Scale transition.

• Keyboard accessible.

⸻

Images

Use fade-in animations when entering the viewport.

Do not animate every image at once.

Use intersection observers where appropriate.

⸻

Video

Hero video:

• Fade in on page load.

• Smooth transition from poster image.

Avoid flashing or abrupt playback.

⸻

Section Reveal

As users scroll:

Reveal sections using:

• Fade up

or

• Gentle fade

Avoid:

• Flip animations

• Bounce effects

• Large rotations

⸻

Footer

Fade into view naturally.

Social icons:

• Gold hover color.

• Slight upward movement.

Newsletter field:

• Smooth focus transition.

⸻

Page Transitions

If routing supports transitions:

Use:

• Fade

• Crossfade

• Slight vertical movement

Maintain fast navigation.

⸻

Performance

Respect the user’s “prefers-reduced-motion” setting.

Disable non-essential animations when reduced motion is enabled.

Use GPU-accelerated transforms.

Avoid layout shifts.

Target 60 FPS on modern devices.

⸻

Accessibility

Animations must never interfere with usability.

Maintain keyboard navigation.

Maintain screen reader compatibility.

No flashing or rapid movement.

⸻

Validation Checklist

Verify that:

• Every animation uses the same timing system.

• Every hover effect is consistent.

• Every page transition feels smooth.

• No animation blocks user interaction.

• Performance remains excellent.

• Lighthouse Performance and Accessibility scores remain high.

The final experience should feel polished, cinematic, and luxurious while remaining fast, responsive, and accessible.
