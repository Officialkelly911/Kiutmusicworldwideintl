KIUT Music Brand Integration — Phase 8

Logo & Brand Asset Integration

Objective

Integrate the official Kiut Music brand identity across the entire website.

Do not redesign the logo.

Do not recreate the logo.

Do not change proportions.

The uploaded master logo is now the official brand identity and must replace every previous logo or placeholder throughout the project.

⸻
