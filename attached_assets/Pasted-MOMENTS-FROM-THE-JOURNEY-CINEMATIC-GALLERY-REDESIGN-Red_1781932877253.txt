MOMENTS FROM THE JOURNEY – CINEMATIC GALLERY REDESIGN

Redesign the “Moments From The Journey” section on the About page to match the visual style shown in the reference video.

Do NOT create a Pinterest-style masonry gallery.

The final experience should feel like:

* a luxury editorial photo archive
* a visual diary
* cinematic
* immersive
* elegant
* modern
* premium
* emotionally warm
* aligned with the dark Kiut Music aesthetic

⸻

SECTION HEADING

Keep:

“MOMENTS FROM THE JOURNEY”

Add a subtle subtitle underneath:

“A visual archive of milestones, studio sessions, performances, and memories behind the music.”

Increase spacing above and below the heading.

Center everything.

⸻

LAYOUT STYLE

Build an editorial collage layout similar to the reference video.

Requirements:

Featured Hero Card

The first image should be much larger than the others and act as the visual anchor.

It should span more space and immediately draw attention.

⸻

Mixed Card Rhythm

Create a varied composition using:

* portrait cards
* landscape cards
* square cards
* occasional larger feature cards

Avoid perfectly identical boxes.

The layout should feel intentionally curated.

⸻

Responsive Behavior

Desktop:

* staggered editorial grid
* asymmetrical but balanced
* strong visual rhythm
* generous spacing

Tablet:

* simplified version of the desktop composition

Mobile:

* stacked layout
* featured image first
* maintain visual hierarchy
* no overflow issues

⸻

CARD STYLING

Images should dominate.

No heavy overlays.

Use:

* border-radius consistent with the site
* subtle shadows
* slight depth
* clean spacing
* overflow hidden

Cards should feel premium.

⸻

IMAGE QUALITY

Preserve image colors.

Do NOT apply gold filters.

Keep photos vibrant and cinematic.

Use only a very subtle bottom gradient when needed.

⸻

HOVER INTERACTIONS

Desktop hover:

* image scale to 1.05
* shadow slightly increases
* very soft gold edge glow
* smooth transitions

Animation duration:

300–500ms

Use easing for natural movement.

Avoid flashy effects.

⸻

SCROLL REVEALS

Animate cards into view.

Use:

* fade in
* translate upward slightly
* stagger timing between cards

Animations should feel cinematic and elegant.

Do not over-animate.

⸻

STORY LABELS

Show labels on selected images only.

Examples:

* Studio Session
* On Stage
* Behind The Scenes
* Release Era
* Family & Roots
* Journey Moment
* Performance Night

Style:

* small pill badges
* subtle gold accent
* semi-transparent background
* elegant typography

Do not place labels on every image.

⸻

PREMIUM LIGHTBOX

Clicking an image should open a fullscreen gallery viewer.

Requirements:

* dark cinematic background
* centered image
* smooth animation
* next and previous controls
* keyboard support
* swipe support on mobile
* close button
* optional caption

The experience should feel immersive.

⸻

DEPTH EFFECT

Create subtle depth by using:

* occasional image offsets
* varied card sizes
* layered spacing

The section should resemble an exhibition wall or visual scrapbook.

⸻

MICRO INTERACTIONS

Add:

* smooth hover transitions
* lazy loading
* image fade-in when loaded
* subtle shadow changes

Everything should feel refined.

⸻

PERFORMANCE

Optimize images.

Use lazy loading.

Prevent layout shift.

Ensure smooth scrolling.

Maintain responsiveness.
