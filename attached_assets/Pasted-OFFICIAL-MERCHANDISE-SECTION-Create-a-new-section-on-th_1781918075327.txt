OFFICIAL MERCHANDISE SECTION

Create a new section on the About page immediately above the footer.

The purpose of this section is to showcase official Kiut merchandise and encourage visitors to visit the external store.

Do NOT embed the external store directly.

Instead, create a premium merchandise showcase section.

⸻

SECTION TITLE

Heading:

OFFICIAL MERCHANDISE

Subtitle:

“Take a piece of the journey with you.”

Center align everything.

Use generous spacing.

Maintain the cinematic and premium aesthetic used throughout the About page.

⸻

SECTION BACKGROUND

Since the About page already uses the full-page video background:

Do NOT create a solid background.

Instead create a floating glass container.

Use:

background: rgba(15,15,18,0.45)

backdrop-filter: blur(16px)

border:

1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.06)

soft shadows

rounded corners

Maintain consistency with the rest of the page.

⸻

MERCH GRID

Create a responsive merchandise grid.

Desktop:

4 cards

Tablet:

2 cards

Mobile:

1 card

Spacing should be generous.

Cards should feel luxurious and modern.

⸻

CARD CONTENT

Each merchandise card should contain:

* product image
* item name
* subtle category label

Examples:

T-Shirt

Hoodie

Poster

Cap

Limited Edition

Accessory

Do NOT show prices.

Keep the presentation editorial and elegant.

⸻

CARD DESIGN

Use:

* rounded corners
* overflow hidden
* soft shadows
* glassmorphism styling
* subtle gold accent details

Cards should feel premium.

⸻

HOVER EFFECTS

Desktop hover:

* image scale 1.05
* slightly stronger shadow
* subtle gold glow
* smooth transition

Animation duration:

300–500ms

Avoid flashy animations.

⸻

SCROLL REVEALS

Animate cards into view.

Use:

* fade up
* staggered timing
* slight translateY

Animations should feel cinematic and smooth.

⸻

FEATURED ITEM

Make one merchandise card slightly larger than the others.

This becomes the hero product.

Maintain visual balance.

⸻

CTA AREA

Below the merchandise cards create a premium CTA block.

Heading:

Wear The Story

Paragraph:

“Explore the official collection and bring the journey beyond the music.”

Primary button:

SHOP THE COLLECTION

The button should open the external store URL in a new tab.

Use:

target=”_blank”

Include:

hover glow

slight lift effect

smooth transitions

⸻

OPTIONAL MICRODETAILS

Add:

small sparkle effects

subtle floating motion

gentle parallax

very soft gold highlights

Keep everything understated.

⸻

RESPONSIVENESS

Desktop:

4-card layout

Tablet:

2-card layout

Mobile:

single-column layout

Prevent overflow.

Maintain spacing.

⸻

PERFORMANCE

Use lazy loading.

Optimize images.

Avoid layout shift.

Keep animations smooth.

⸻

DESIGN RESTRICTIONS

Do NOT:

* embed the external store in an iframe
* show product prices
* overcrowd the section
* use loud animations
* break the dark cinematic aesthetic

Prioritize:

* elegance
* storytelling
* premium presentation
* brand consistency
* emotional connection

⸻

FINAL EXPERIENCE

The merchandise section should feel like an extension of Kiut’s world rather than a separate store.

Visitors should feel invited into the collection, not redirected to a random shopping page.

After implementation summarize:

1. Files modified.
2. Components changed.
3. Animation system used.
4. Mobile behavior.
5. External store link integration.
