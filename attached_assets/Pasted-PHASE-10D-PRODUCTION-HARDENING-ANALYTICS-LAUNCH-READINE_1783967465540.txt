PHASE 10D — PRODUCTION HARDENING, ANALYTICS & LAUNCH READINESS

OBJECTIVE

Complete the final production configuration for the Kiut Music website.

Do NOT redesign any UI.

Do NOT change layouts, spacing, colors, typography, animations, CTA components, navigation, or branding.

This phase focuses exclusively on performance, reliability, SEO, analytics, accessibility, monitoring, security, and launch preparation.

--------------------------------------------------
1. ENVIRONMENT VALIDATION
--------------------------------------------------

Verify all environment variables.

Confirm graceful handling if any variable is missing.

Validate:

RESEND_API_KEY

CONTACT_FROM_EMAIL

MAILCHIMP_API_KEY

MAILCHIMP_SERVER_PREFIX
