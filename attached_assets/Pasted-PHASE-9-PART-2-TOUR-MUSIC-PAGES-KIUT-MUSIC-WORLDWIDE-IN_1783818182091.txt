PHASE 9 — PART 2

TOUR & MUSIC PAGES

KIUT MUSIC WORLDWIDE INTERNATIONAL

Master Replit Implementation Specification

⸻

OBJECTIVE

This phase completes the Tour and Music pages using the approved Kiut Music design system established in previous phases.

This is an implementation phase, not a redesign phase.

The existing visual identity, navigation, reusable CTA buttons, branding, typography, hero system, spacing, animations, and component library are considered final and must remain unchanged.

Every new section should reuse existing components wherever possible.

The completed pages should feel like they belong to one continuous premium digital experience.

⸻

GLOBAL REQUIREMENTS

Before implementing any section:

* Preserve the existing navigation.
* Preserve the premium CTA button component exactly as implemented.
* Preserve the header and footer.
* Preserve the approved logo system.
* Preserve the universal Hero component.
* Preserve responsive image architecture.
* Preserve the existing animation language.
* Preserve all previously approved spacing, typography, and color systems.

No redesigns are permitted.
