ACCEPTANCE CHECKLIST

* Double opt-in functions correctly.
* Welcome emails are delivered.
* Contact emails are delivered.
* Loading, success, and error states work correctly.

Implementation rule: This is a functionality upgrade, not a redesign. The existing luxury aesthetic, animations, branding, spacing, typography, responsive hero system, reusable CTA components, and overall user experience established in previous phases must remain visually identical while the Connect and Newsletter features become fully production-ready.
