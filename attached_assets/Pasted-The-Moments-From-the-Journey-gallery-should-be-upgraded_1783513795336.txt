Transform the existing gallery into a modern, premium, cinematic image experience by adding refined hover animations and a full-screen lightbox viewer.

Do not redesign the gallery layout.

Maintain the existing premium black-and-gold aesthetic.

PART 1 – PREMIUM HOVER ANIMATIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Enhance every gallery card with subtle, high-end interactions.

On hover:

• Smooth image zoom (scale between 1.03–1.08)
• Soft gold border glow
• Slight elevation (translateY: -4px)
• Shadow intensity increases subtly
• Image brightness increases slightly
• Labels gently fade upward
• Cursor changes to indicate interactivity

Animation timing:

• Duration: 250–350ms
• Easing: ease-out or cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)

Avoid exaggerated movement.

The interaction should feel elegant and luxurious.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PART 2 – CINEMATIC LIGHTBOX
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

When a gallery image is clicked:

Open a full-screen modal lightbox.

The lightbox should:

• Fade in smoothly
• Darken the entire background
• Focus attention on the selected image
• Prevent background scrolling
• Close smoothly

Use a soft backdrop blur where supported.
