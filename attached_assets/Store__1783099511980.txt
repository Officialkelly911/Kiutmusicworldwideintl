Homepage store CTA Section Improvement Audit
KIUT MUSIC WEBSITE
Store CTA Section – Premium Upgrade Audit
Project Goal
Transform the current Store CTA section on the Home page into a premium artist merchandise showcase that feels luxurious, cinematic, and exclusive while remaining responsive and performance-friendly.
 
⸻
 
1. Make Merchandise the Hero
Current Issue
The merchandise images occupy too little space inside their containers, making the products appear secondary.
Improvements
	•	Increase image size by approximately 30–50%.
	•	Allow products to fill 75–85% of the card.
	•	Reduce unnecessary internal padding.
	•	Preserve image quality and aspect ratio.
	•	Use object-fit: contain or adjust layout appropriately so artwork remains fully visible.
 
⸻
 
2. Improve Product Card Design
Redesign each merchandise card to feel premium.
Add:
	•	Rounded corners
	•	Soft shadows
	•	Thin gold accent borders
	•	Premium dark background
	•	Better internal spacing
	•	Consistent card heights
Cards should resemble luxury product displays rather than standard e-commerce cards.
 
⸻
 
3. Create Better Product Hierarchy
Each card should display:
	•	Product Badge (optional)
	•	Large Product Image
	•	Product Name
	•	Short Description
	•	Price
	•	CTA Button
This creates a cleaner visual flow.
 
⸻
 
4. Add Product Badges
Introduce tasteful labels such as:
	•	NEW
	•	LIMITED EDITION
	•	BEST SELLER
	•	EXCLUSIVE
	•	PREMIUM
	•	SIGNED
Badges should be small, elegant, and styled with subtle gold accents.
 
⸻
 
5. Upgrade Hover Effects
Desktop hover interactions should include:
	•	Product image zoom (1.03–1.05)
	•	Card lift
	•	Slight gold glow
	•	Stronger shadow
	•	Smooth transitions (300–500ms)
Avoid excessive animations.
 
⸻
 
6. Strengthen CTA Buttons
Current buttons should become more visually engaging.
Suggestions:
	•	Shop Now
	•	Explore Collection
	•	View Collection
	•	View Product
Enhancements:
	•	Larger height
	•	Rounded corners
	•	Gold gradient or outline
	•	Soft hover glow
	•	Slight upward movement
 
⸻
 
7. Premium Background Treatment
Replace flat backgrounds with:
	•	Dark radial gradients
	•	Soft spotlight behind products
	•	Subtle texture
	•	Gentle lighting effects
Maintain a clean luxury appearance.
 
⸻
 
8. Improve Spacing
Increase:
	•	Vertical spacing
	•	Image breathing room
	•	Text spacing
	•	Card separation
Luxury design relies on generous whitespace.
 
⸻
 
9. Introduce a Featured Product
Highlight one merchandise item.
Display:
	•	Larger image
	•	Featured badge
	•	Premium styling
	•	Slightly larger card
Use this to promote the newest or most important item.
 
⸻
 
10. Editorial Layout
Avoid identical cards.
Create visual rhythm by mixing:
	•	Large cards
	•	Medium cards
	•	Standard cards
This creates a curated fashion editorial feel.
 
⸻
 
11. Scroll Reveal Animations
Animate products using:
	•	Fade in
	•	Slight upward movement
	•	Staggered timing
Duration:
0.6–0.8 seconds
 
⸻
 
12. Merchandise Spotlight
Introduce an automatic spotlight effect.
Every few seconds:
	•	One product receives a soft glow.
	•	Card enlarges slightly.
	•	“Featured This Week” appears.
	•	Spotlight transitions smoothly to another item.
This creates subtle motion without distracting users.
 
⸻
 
13. Featured Collection Banner
Place a premium promotional banner above the merchandise grid.
Include:
	•	Collection title
	•	Short description
	•	One large featured product image
	•	CTA button
Purpose:
Introduce visitors to the collection before they browse.
 
⸻
 
14. Carousel Section
Add a secondary section near the bottom.
Examples:
Fans Also Love
Recently Added
More From KiutRaba
Display products in a smooth horizontal scrolling carousel.
 
⸻
 
15. Performance
Ensure:
	•	Lazy-loaded images
	•	Optimized graphics
	•	Smooth animations
	•	No layout shifts
	•	Fast loading on desktop and mobile
 
⸻
 
Final Objective
The Store CTA section should feel like a premium artist collection showroom rather than a traditional online store.
Visitors should immediately perceive exclusivity, quality, and strong brand identity.
