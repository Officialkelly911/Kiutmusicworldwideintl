# Brand Identity

## Brand Personality

Kiut is presented as:

- Cinematic
- Confident
- Elegant
- Creative
- Modern
- Authentic
- Ambitious
- Emotionally expressive

The brand should feel premium without feeling distant.

---

## Visual Keywords

- Midnight Black
- Gold Accents
- Editorial Layouts
- Soft Lighting
- Generous Spacing
- Film-Like Composition
- Minimal Interface
- Smooth Motion

---

## Audience

Primary audience:

- Music fans
- Visual content consumers
- Streaming platform users
- Concert attendees
- Merchandise buyers
- Creative industry professionals

Secondary audience:

- Potential collaborators
- Event organizers
- Media outlets
- Brand partners

---

## Tone of Voice

The website copy should be:

- Clear
- Confident
- Concise
- Artistic
- Welcoming
- Never overly corporate
- Never overly casual

---

## Design Inspirations

The visual direction draws inspiration from:

- Apple product storytelling
- Spotify music experiences
- Netflix cinematic presentation
- Luxury fashion editorial layouts
- Modern artist portfolio websites

---

## Brand Promise

Every page should communicate that Kiut is not only creating music, but creating experiences, memories, and visual stories that connect with people on a deeper level.
