# Design Philosophy

## Core Principle

Luxury is created through consistency, restraint, and attention to detail.

The Kiut Music website should feel intentional rather than crowded.

---

## Principle 1 — Music First

The interface exists to support the music, not compete with it.

Visual elements should guide attention toward listening, watching, and exploring.

---

## Principle 2 — Cinematic Storytelling

Every page should feel like a scene from a music film.

Motion, lighting, spacing, and imagery should work together to create atmosphere.

---

## Principle 3 — Consistency Over Novelty

Reusable patterns are preferred over constantly introducing new visual styles.

Consistency increases perceived quality.

---

## Principle 4 — Purposeful Motion

Animations should have a reason to exist.

Motion may be used to:

- Guide attention
- Indicate hierarchy
- Provide feedback
- Create continuity
- Enhance storytelling

Motion should never feel distracting.

---

## Principle 5 — Mobile First

The primary experience is mobile.

Every feature must work beautifully on small screens before desktop enhancements are added.

---

## Principle 6 — Breathing Room

Generous spacing is a feature, not wasted space.

Whitespace helps the content feel premium and easier to consume.

---

## Principle 7 — Performance Matters

A beautiful experience that loads slowly is not a successful experience.

Optimize images, videos, animations, and interactions for smooth performance.

---

## Non-Negotiables

- Use only approved brand colors.
- Use only approved typography scales.
- Keep border radius values consistent.
- Maintain the black-and-gold visual identity.
- Avoid unnecessary visual clutter.
- Ensure every interactive element provides visible feedback.
- Prioritize responsiveness and accessibility on every page.
